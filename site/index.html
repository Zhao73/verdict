Ask about a stock the way you'd ask a colleague. How it works工作方式

Code fetches the facts first, models research and argue, code does the math, and code assembles the report, so nothing gets lost in a summary.代码先抓数据，模型负责研究和辩论，计算交给代码，报告也由代码拼装，任何内容都不会在总结时丢失。

01 · SNAPSHOT

Live data in seconds几秒拿到实时数据

Price, history, filings, fundamentals, options and dated news, fetched in parallel before any model runs.价格、走势、公告、财务、期权和带日期的新闻，在模型运行前并行抓取。

02 · DESKS

Four analysts in parallel四位分析师并行

Business, expectations, news and risk desks, each running about five targeted searches and steered by your question.业务、预期、新闻、风险四个研究台，每个约 5 次针对性搜索，重点跟着你的问题走。

03 · DEBATE

Bull vs bear多空辩论

The strongest long and short cases, argued from the record, then a portfolio manager decides while you watch.多头和空头基于研究记录各自论证，投资经理当着你的面写下结论。

04 · VERDICT

A verdict you can act on拿来就能用的结论

Rating, value range, price levels to avoid, start or add, catalysts, risks and what would prove it wrong.评级、估值区间、回避 / 建仓 / 加仓的价格条件、催化剂、风险，以及什么情况说明判断错了。

05 · RECEIPTS

Every claim has a source每条结论都有出处

Every finding links to its source. Missing data is listed, never guessed, and a run that did not finish says so.每条发现都能追溯到来源；缺失的数据会列出，绝不靠猜；没跑完的研究会明确标出。

06 · EVERYWHERE

Terminal, Claude Code, Codex终端、Claude Code、Codex

A full-screen app and a stream mode, plus plugins where subagents research in parallel. One history across all of them.全屏应用和命令行两种模式，外加插件（子代理并行研究），研究历史在各入口之间共享。

Verdict methods: code does the mathVerdict 独家方法：计算交给代码

Seven deterministic methods frame the thesis before the models write and audit the verdict after. They roll up into a 0–100 Verdict Score that cross-checks the rating and never overrides it.七种方法由代码按固定规则计算：模型动笔前先给论点定好框架，结论出来后再逐条检验，最后汇总成 0–100 的 Verdict 评分。评分用来交叉检验评级，不会改动评级。

BEFORE
Priced-in growth价格隐含的增长Reverse DCF: the growth today's price already assumes, compared with what the business delivered.反向 DCF：现价已经假设了多高的增长，再和公司过去的实际增速对比。
BEFORE
Options-implied move期权隐含波动The move the options market prices, and whether that is more than recent history.期权市场预期的波动区间，以及它是否高于近期的实际波动。
BEFORE
Tape走势状态Trend × volatility regime, with what it means for timing.趋势和波动组合出的市场状态，以及它对择时意味着什么。
AFTER
Zone odds触及概率The chance the price actually reaches each price level within three months.3 个月内股价走到每个价位区间的概率。
AFTER
Payoff收益风险Scenario-weighted value, upside vs downside and reward-to-risk.情景加权价值、上行与下行空间、收益/风险比。
AFTER
Evidence balance & audit证据天平与一致性检查Findings weighted by source quality; a rating that contradicts the evidence is flagged ⚠.按来源质量给研究发现加权；评级和证据方向相反时用 ⚠ 标出。
